State Social Studies Standards — Grade 2 (Sample Reference)

Domain: Geography
2.G.1 — Use maps, photographs, and other tools to identify and describe places in the local
community and how those places meet people's needs.
2.G.2 — Explain how the local environment (weather, land, water) affects how people in the
community live, work, and travel.
2.G.3 — Describe reasons people settle in particular places, including access to resources and
transportation routes.

Domain: Civics
2.C.1 — Identify a problem in the classroom, school, or community and compare different people's
points of view on the problem.
2.C.2 — Describe examples of civic participation, past and present.

Domain: Economics
2.E.1 — Describe how people in the local community produce, exchange, and use goods and services.
