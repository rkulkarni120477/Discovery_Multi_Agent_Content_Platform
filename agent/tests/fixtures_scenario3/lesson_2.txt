Lesson 2: Where People Live and Why (Grade 2, Unit 3)

Driving Question: Why do people choose to live where they live?
Learning Target: I can describe reasons a community formed in a particular place.

Vocabulary: settlement, resource, transportation route.

Student Activity: Students examine a simple map of a fictional town showing a river, a forest,
and a road, and discuss in pairs why the town might have been built where it was. Students then
draw their own map of an imaginary settlement and label two reasons people might choose to live
there.

Concepts Developed: Access to resources (water, land) and transportation routes influence where
communities form.

Standards Claimed (teacher notes): D2.Geo.2.K-2, D2.Geo.5.K-2

Assessment: Completed map with two labeled reasons for the settlement's location.

Hook Strategy: Show a photograph of a town built next to a river and ask "why here?"

Timing: 40 minutes.
