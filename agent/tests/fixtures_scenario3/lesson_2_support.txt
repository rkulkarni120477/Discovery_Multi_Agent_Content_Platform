Lesson 2 Educator Support: Where People Live and Why

Facilitation Notes: Before the lesson, print one map handout per pair. Circulate during the
paired discussion to prompt students who focus only on the river ("what else do you notice near
the town?").

Sample Student Response: "The town is next to the river because people need water to drink and to
grow food. The road lets people bring goods to the town."

Differentiation: For students who need more support, provide a sentence frame: "The town is near
___ because people need ___."

Materials: Map handout, colored pencils, photograph of a riverside town.
