Grade 2 Essentials Scope and Sequence — Social Studies

Unit 3: Human-Environment Interaction

This unit explores how people affect and are affected by their physical environment, using maps
and geographic tools to understand human settlement patterns.

Lesson 2: Where People Live and Why
Standards claimed: D2.Geo.2.K-2 — Use maps, graphs, photographs, and other representations to
describe places and the relationships and interactions that shape them.

Lesson 3: Changing the Land
Standards claimed: D2.Geo.4.K-2 — Explain how weather, climate, and other environmental
characteristics affect people's lives in a place or region.

Lesson 4: Moving People and Goods
Standards claimed: D2.Geo.5.K-2 — Describe how environmental and cultural characteristics
influence population distribution in specific places or regions.

Lesson 5: Working Together as Citizens
Standards claimed: D2.Civ.10.K-2 — Compare their own point of view with others' perspectives.
