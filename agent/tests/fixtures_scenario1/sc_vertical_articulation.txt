South Carolina Vertical Articulation of SEPs, DCIs, and CCCs (Reference Excerpt), Grades K-5

Grade 3 expectations for the Science and Engineering Practice "Planning and Carrying Out
Investigations": students should independently plan simple investigations with multiple trials and
identify variables to control, building on Grade 2's teacher-guided investigations.

Grade 3 expectations for the Disciplinary Core Idea "Types of Interactions" (forces): students
should describe cause-and-effect relationships for both contact and non-contact forces (magnetism)
using firsthand observation, at a descriptive (not yet quantitative) level.

Grade 4 expectations for the Disciplinary Core Idea "Energy and Energy Transfer": students should
move beyond simply observing energy transfer (Grade 3-4 baseline) to modeling energy transfer
through devices, including simple energy-conversion devices, as a bridge toward Grade 5's more
quantitative treatment.
