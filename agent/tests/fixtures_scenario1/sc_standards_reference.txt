South Carolina Science Standards (Reference Excerpt), Grades 3-4

3.P.2A.1: Conduct controlled investigations, using multiple trials, to determine the effect of
unbalanced forces on the speed or direction of an object's motion. (Clarification: emphasis on
multiple-trial data collection, an addition beyond the NGSS reference.)

3.P.2A.3: Analyze and interpret data from observations to describe patterns of interactions
between magnets and other magnets or objects.

4.P.3A.2: Develop and use models to describe that energy can be transferred from one place to
another by sound, light, heat, or electric currents, or through the use of a device that converts
one form of energy into another (e.g., a windmill converts wind energy into electrical energy).
(Clarification: explicit inclusion of energy-conversion devices, an elaboration beyond the NGSS
reference.)
