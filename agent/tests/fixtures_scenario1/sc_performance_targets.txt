South Carolina Performance Targets (Reference Excerpt), Grades 3-4

Target for 3.P.2A.1 (Meets): The student conducts a controlled investigation using multiple
trials and accurately describes the effect of unbalanced forces on an object's speed or direction,
citing specific trial data. Requires observable student performance (a conducted investigation
with recorded data), not just verbal recall.

Target for 3.P.2A.3 (Meets): The student analyzes a data set of magnet-object interactions and
correctly describes at least two patterns observed. Requires observable student performance
(analysis of a provided or collected data set).

Target for 4.P.3A.2 (Meets): The student develops a model showing energy transfer through at least
one device that converts one energy form to another, and explains the conversion in the model's
key. Requires observable student performance (a constructed model).
